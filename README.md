## CHAPTER 1.2

Separate R Scripts and datasets are held for analyses of different databases. All analyses require `arthropods_genus.nwk` which contains full arthropod phylogeny, at genus level, held in TimeTree (accessed: 07/08/2025). `Families_Aquatic_Check.xlsx` shows the families identified within TimeTree as aquatic to be included in this analysis. 

**Australia**: `Australia_Database_Analysis.R` and `Australia_Data_Clean.csv`

**China**: `China_Data_Analysis.R` and `China_Data.csv`

**Europe**: `Europe_Database_Analysis.R` and `Europe_Data_Clean.csv`

**New Zealand**: `NZ_Database_Analysis.R` and `NZ_Data_Raw.csv`

**South Africa**: `SA_Database_Analysis.R` and `SA_Database.csv`

**USA**: `US_Database_Analysis.R`, `US_TSN_Data.csv` and `US_Trait_Data.csv`
